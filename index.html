Questions and answers

Get the facts.

What will voters decide?

Charlottesville voters will consider whether to approve a 1% local sales tax dedicated to funding school construction and modernization. Localities across Virginia are considering similar measures.

When is the vote?

The proposal will appear on the November 3, 2026 ballot for City of Charlottesville voters.

How long would the funding last?

A yes vote would mean the 1% increase starts in January 2027 and extend through June 2046, creating a dedicated 20-year funding source.

Would the tax apply to groceries or medicine?

No. The ballot measure states that groceries, essential personal hygiene products, and prescription medications would be exempt.
